BEHIND THE NUMBERS

A score is a perspective.
Not the whole story.

Each genre’s score is the average of its individual user ratings. A movie can belong to more than one genre, so its ratings may contribute to several categories.

Small samples can tell a different story. Check the rating count alongside each score; genres without ratings are shown as “Not rated yet.”
